- Native CSRF retained, original inputs preserved and session locking required.
- Durable replay reservations, Blade fields and cacheable configuration.
- Maintenance commands, EN/FR messages and HTTP/JSON error handling.
- Integration/concurrency tests and Composer installation/removal checks.
